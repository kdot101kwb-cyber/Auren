# NOVA USDT Opportunity Verification Policy

## Goal
Find income opportunities that a Sudan-based user can actually use, with no signup fee, deposit, paid membership, or required purchase, and that pay USDT on a network the user can receive. Optimize for expected net earnings per hour, not advertised headline income.

## Hard safety gates
Reject or hold an opportunity if any of these are true:
- Sudan eligibility is prohibited, unclear, or only inferred from "global" marketing copy.
- The platform requires an upfront payment, deposit, activation fee, paid training, or buying a product to unlock work.
- The platform requires false location, VPN-based eligibility evasion, another person's identity/payment account, or misleading KYC details.
- Payout network, minimum withdrawal, withdrawal fee, account verification, or country-specific payout access is unknown.
- The work is illegal, deceptive engagement, fake reviews, account rental, credential sharing, or requests for wallet seed phrases/private keys.
- The only payment evidence is a platform's own payout counter, testimonials, screenshots, or affiliate article.

## Evidence levels
1. `unverified`: opportunity discovered; not yet checked.
2. `platformClaim`: official site claims payment or global access, but eligibility/payment has not been independently corroborated.
3. `eligibilityChecked`: official terms or written support confirm Sudan-based registration, work, KYC and withdrawal eligibility; this does not prove payment.
4. `userDocumented`: user has a genuine completed-work and received-transaction record, with private information redacted.
5. `independentlyVerified`: credible independent evidence of actual payouts plus current eligibility and withdrawal terms. Do not use this label based on platform marketing alone.
6. `paidOutVerified`: the user completed a small, no-cost test and received the payout in their own wallet; store transaction hash and date only with user consent.

## Required fields for every candidate
- Official opportunity URL and official terms URL
- Work type, skills, estimated time, gross pay, expected net pay/hour
- Country eligibility specifically for a resident of Sudan
- KYC requirements and supported identity documents
- USDT network(s), minimum withdrawal, platform fee, network fee, and payout schedule
- No-startup-cost check
- Independent payment evidence URL(s), publication date and evidence quality
- Last checked timestamp, expiry/recheck date, unresolved questions, confidence and rejection reasons

## Ranking
Use hard gates first; do not let a high score override a failed country, fee, or payment gate. Among eligible candidates, rank by:
1. verified access from Sudan and verified payout path;
2. net expected earnings per hour after fees;
3. likelihood of getting accepted and receiving enough work;
4. first-payout speed and minimum withdrawal;
5. quality and recency of independent evidence.

Unknown rates must remain unknown; never fabricate income estimates. Show low/base/high estimates only when supported by actual rates and clearly stated assumptions.

## Candidate notes at initial research
- WorkChain (workchain.cloud): official page advertises USDT-TRC20, $10 minimum withdrawal, about $1 network fee, and says workers outside its five named off-ramp countries may still work. These remain platform claims until current Sudan eligibility and a real payout are checked.
- InstaDo: official pages advertise worldwide USDT transactions and a safe-deal workflow; its terms list several non-USDT withdrawal fees and the exact USDT network/fee was not established. Treat Sudan access and USDT withdrawal details as unresolved.
- OKX Orbit: requires KYC and eligible original crypto content; rewards are variable, not guaranteed. Sudan eligibility for the account and creator program must be confirmed before work begins.
- Shoot: exclude for a Sudan-resident account because the current terms list Sudan as restricted for payouts.
- Do not classify any platform as guaranteed to pay before the user receives a real payout. Even one successful payout proves only that specific transaction, not future earnings.

## NOVA operating workflow
1. Discover candidates from official pages, terms, help centers and credible independent sources.
2. Check current Sudan eligibility, no-cost entry, payout method, network, thresholds and all fees.
3. Ask support a precise written question when any critical detail is ambiguous; save the response date and URL or user-provided evidence.
4. Present only a short ranked shortlist with evidence and unresolved risks. Label all claims and dates.
5. Before the user invests significant time, recommend the smallest realistic no-cost paid task and a test withdrawal, if available.
6. After the user reports receipt, optionally record a redacted transaction hash and date with consent.
7. Recheck eligibility and fees periodically; mark stale records rather than silently treating old terms as current.
8. Never create accounts, submit KYC, accept jobs, publish content, or move funds without the user's explicit approval.

## Current status
This file defines the verification policy and initial leads; it does not implement an automatic live crawler, prove that any listed platform accepts Sudan residents, or prove any payout. Those capabilities require separate implementation, testing, and current evidence.
