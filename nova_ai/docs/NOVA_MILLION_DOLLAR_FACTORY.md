# NOVA AI Million Dollar Factory

**Status:** Foundation implementation — offline workspace only.  
**Goal:** Build a portfolio of small, evidence-led AI-enabled businesses. A million dollars is an aspirational long-term target, not a forecast or promise.

## Operating principles

- Start at USD 0: no paid subscriptions, credits, deposits, paid applications, or domain purchases.
- Validate demand before building a SaaS or scaling a content network.
- Record received revenue separately from quotes, pipeline, projections, and pending invoices.
- Confirm country eligibility, KYC, payment rails, minimum payout, fees, and withdrawal availability for Sudan before committing work.
- Do not use crypto rails or stablecoins as a default payout assumption. Check applicable law and official platform terms first.
- No automatic applications, outreach, public publishing, contract acceptance, purchases, trading, or transfers.
- Keep credentials and provider secrets off-device and out of source control.

## Portfolio engines

1. **Service Studio:** human-reviewed AI-assisted services for immediate validation.
2. **Content Factory:** original short-form content, series, and virtual characters after pilot quality is proven.
3. **Micro-SaaS Lab:** small tools built only after repeated buyer demand is documented.
4. **Digital Products:** original templates, guides, and reusable assets with verified distribution/payment support.
5. **AI Agent Agency:** scoped automation and knowledge assistants for clients, using approved APIs and secure server-side credentials.
6. **Global Sales & Opportunity Radar:** research, lead qualification, eligibility checks, and draft proposals.

## First pilot — Sudanese Arabic AI localization and quality review

This is a test hypothesis, not a claim of professional certification or guaranteed demand.

**Potential buyers:** AI data teams, language technology vendors, app developers, and content teams that need Sudanese Arabic review.

**Initial deliverable:** a small original sample showing source text, AI output, corrected Sudanese Arabic, issue tags (meaning, tone, dialect, terminology), and a short rationale.

**Validation gates:**
1. Produce a 10-line sample without using confidential or copyrighted client data.
2. Have the sample reviewed for accuracy and naturalness; correct weak areas.
3. Find five real buyers and verify the buyer's location, current need, eligibility, payment method, fees, and timing.
4. Send nothing until the owner approves the exact message and recipient.
5. Offer a small paid pilot only after scope, rights, price, delivery format, and payment terms are explicit.
6. Record revenue only after funds are actually received.

## Unit economics

For every offer, track:
- time spent by task;
- delivered quantity and acceptance criteria;
- gross amount agreed;
- platform/payment fees;
- net amount actually received;
- effective earnings per working hour;
- refunds, revisions, and unpaid work.

Never convert per-audio-hour, per-task, or per-project rates into hourly earnings without measured working time.

## Release stages

- **Stage 1:** offline offer, checklist, lead, and received-payment workspace.
- **Stage 2:** verified public opportunity sources and country/payment eligibility evidence.
- **Stage 3:** sample portfolio and owner-approved outreach drafts.
- **Stage 4:** secure client/project workflow and delivery QA.
- **Stage 5:** expand to content, digital products, agents, and micro-SaaS only when evidence supports it.

## Current implementation boundary

The first screen stores checklist progress, lead notes, and manually entered received payments on the device using SharedPreferences. It does not browse the web, contact buyers, generate media, invoice, process payments, or synchronize data to a server. A displayed lead is not a contacted lead; a manually entered amount is not independently verified by NOVA.
